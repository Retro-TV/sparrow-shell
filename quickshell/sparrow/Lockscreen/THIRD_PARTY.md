# Lockscreen third-party assets

## Qylock “Last of Us” theme

The lockscreen composition in `LastOfUs.qml` is adapted from the **Last of Us**
theme by Darkkal44 in [qylock](https://github.com/Darkkal44/qylock/tree/main/themes/last-of-us).
The original QML is GPL-3.0; its license is included as
[`COPYING.GPL-3.0`](COPYING.GPL-3.0). Sparrow's adaptation replaces the SDDM
authentication/session wrapper with Sparrow's existing Quickshell session-lock
and PAM flow, and replaces the bundled background video with Sparrow's selected
per-output wallpaper.
